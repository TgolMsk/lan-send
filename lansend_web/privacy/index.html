LanSend 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-09-08

LanSend 是一款在同一局域网内直接互传文件与剪贴板内容的工具。它不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息。

  • 没有服务器：文件和剪贴板内容只在你的设备与你选定的对方设备之间通过局域网直接传输（TLS 加密），不经过任何第三方服务器，我们也无法访问这些内容。
  • 不收集数据：应用没有账号系统，不采集设备标识符、位置、通讯录、使用统计或崩溃报告，不包含任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。
  • 本机存储：设置、传输历史和剪贴板历史只保存在你的设备本地，可随时在应用内删除。
  • 本地网络权限：应用需要“本地网络”权限来发现同一网络中的设备；该权限只用于此目的。
  • 本地网络：应用在你的 Wi‑Fi 内以组播公告自己的名字、设备类型与端口，并监听同一端口接收传输；这些信息只在局域网内传播，不会发到互联网。
  • 相册（iOS）：选择照片时使用系统照片选择器，应用只拿到你选中的那几张，不会读取整个相册。
  • 联系我们：电子邮件 511297735@qq.com，或 https://github.com/TgolMsk/lan-send/issues

LanSend Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-08

LanSend transfers files and clipboard content directly between devices on the same local network. It does not collect, store or upload any personal data.

  • No servers. Files and clipboard content travel directly between your device and the device you chose, over the local network, encrypted with TLS. Nothing passes through third-party servers, and we have no access to it.
  • No data collection. There are no accounts, no device identifiers, location, contacts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • Local storage only. Settings, transfer history and clipboard history stay on your device and can be deleted in the app at any time.
  • Local network permission is used solely to find other devices on the same network.
  • Local network: the app announces its name, device type and port to your Wi‑Fi by multicast and listens on that port for transfers; this never leaves the local network.
  • Photos (iOS): picking photos uses the system photo picker, so the app only receives the items you select and never reads your whole library.
